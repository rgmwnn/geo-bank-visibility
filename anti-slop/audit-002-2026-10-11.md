# antislop audit 002: Favoured Banks deck (2026-10-11)

Mode: during (session override). RG asked for the deck to stop reading as AI-designed and for readable charts, with antislop core and antislop-copywriting. RG asked to fix, so every finding below is fixed in the same pass.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks and copy patterns = LOW.

## Design

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
